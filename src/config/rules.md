# 🎮 VALORANT TOURNAMENT RULEBOOK

## 📌 General Rules
• All participants must maintain good sportsmanship.
• Toxicity, harassment, and abusive language are strictly prohibited.
• Tournament admins reserve the right to make final decisions on all disputes.
• Violation of rules may result in warnings, match forfeits, or disqualification.

## 👥 Team Requirements
• Maximum 32 teams are allowed.
• Each team must consist of 5 main players.
• Up to 2 substitute players are permitted.
• Players may represent only one team throughout the tournament.
• Smurf accounts and account sharing are prohibited.

## ⏰ Match Timing
• Teams must be ready 15 minutes before their scheduled match.
• Teams failing to appear within 10 minutes of the scheduled start time will receive a forfeit loss.
• Match result screenshots must be submitted after every game.

# 🏆 Tournament Format

### Round of 32
• Single Elimination
• Best of 1 (BO1)

### Round of 16
• Double Elimination
• Teams are eliminated after losing two brackets

### Quarter Finals
• Best of 3 (BO3)

### Semi Finals
• Best of 3 (BO3)

### Grand Finals
• Best of 5 (BO5)

## 🗺️ Map Veto System

### BO1
1. Teams alternate banning maps.
2. The remaining map will be played.

### BO3
1. Team A bans one map.
2. Team B bans one map.
3. Team A picks Map 1.
4. Team B picks Map 2.
5. The remaining map becomes the decider.

### BO5
1. Team A bans one map.
2. Team B bans one map.
3. Teams alternate picking maps.
4. The remaining map becomes the decider.

## 🚫 Prohibited Actions
❌ Cheats, hacks, scripts, or third-party software.

❌ Smurfing or account sharing.

❌ Stream sniping.

❌ Match fixing or collusion.

❌ Intentional exploitation of bugs.

Violations may result in immediate disqualification.

## 🔌 Technical Issues
• Remakes may be approved for disconnects before Round 3.
• Disconnects after Round 3 will generally not result in a restart.
• Players are responsible for maintaining a stable internet connection.

## 📸 Disputes & Evidence
• Any protest must include screenshots or video evidence.
• Admin decisions are final.
• False accusations may lead to penalties.
